    WHAT THIS TOOL DOES

    Useful signals, with limits made clear.

    Paradox Doctor combines deterministic checks with clearly labelled heuristics. A clean scan is not proof that a mod is bug-free.

    01

    Parse the project

    Inspect text-based mod files, localization, IDs, references and game-specific structures.

    02

    Reduce log noise

    Collapse repeated engine messages and preserve the most useful source location we can extract.

    03

    Prioritize action

    High-confidence structural problems appear before low-confidence heuristics and informational notes.
