PRIVACY

Your mod files stay on your device.

Current beta

Paradox Doctor performs scanning in JavaScript inside your browser. The current site does not upload the mod files or error.log contents you select.

Recent scan history stores only small metadata such as timestamp, health score and issue counts in your browser's local storage. It does not store the selected file contents.

Third-party hosting

The website is hosted with GitHub Pages. Normal web hosting infrastructure may process standard connection information needed to deliver the page. Review GitHub's own privacy documentation for its platform practices.

Future analytics or monetization

If analytics, advertising, accounts or paid services are added later, this page should be updated before those features launch so users can understand what data is processed.
