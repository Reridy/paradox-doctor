VICTORIA 3

Victoria 3 mod diagnostics focused on the errors people actually hit

The Victoria 3 profile targets localization/path mistakes, Jomini scope failures, journal/event structure, duplicate IDs, state-region conflicts and repeated error.log noise. It is designed as a zero-install first-pass diagnostic, not a replacement for full engine validation.

Checks included

  • Brace and parser structure checks
  • Localization format, BOM, path and duplicate-key checks
  • Duplicate journal-entry keys and event IDs
  • Missing localization candidates for journal content
  • Duplicate state-region keys and repeated province membership
  • error.log grouping for wrong/unset scopes, event-target failures, unknown commands and missing references
